04 // STUDIO PROFILE

Physical tactile sensibilities engineered into kinetic digital form.

Clamp is an independent design laboratory directed by motion designers, 3D artists, and creative technologists. We reject sterile minimalism in favor of visceral textures, mechanical charms, physical weight, and micro-interactions that leave a permanent impression.

10+ Years Exploring 3D & Motion
40+ Brand Universes Launched
9 International Design Nominations

CORE CAPABILITIES

01

Motion Direction & Kinetic Systems

Physics-based interaction, title sequences, 3D character animation, kinetic typographic branding, and dynamic video loops.

02

3D Art Direction & Industrial Modeling

Hard-surface props, tactile materials, procedural shaders, subsurface scattering, hard plastic/acrylic optics, and cinematic lighting.

03

Brand Identity & Physical Merchandise

Tactile stationery, embossed keytags, apparel, packaging, spatial signage, and comprehensive visual style guides.

04

Creative Engineering & Interactive Web

High-performance canvas physics, WebGL shaders, responsive fluid typography, and bespoke experimental web portfolios.
